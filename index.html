Frequently Asked Questions

Important questions for every client.

Is the 50% advance refundable?

The standard policy is that the 50% project advance is non-refundable after project work or reserved project resources have started, subject to the written agreement, applicable law and any mandatory statutory rights.

When do I pay the remaining 50%?

The remaining 50% is payable after the agreed work/milestone is completed and approved, or according to the written quotation/invoice.

How many days does delivery take?

Delivery depends on pages, features, content, integrations and client feedback. The confirmed timeline is mentioned in the quotation/project agreement.

Do you work with international clients?

Yes. ARB Globle IT & Service is designed to serve clients internationally. Currency display, international support and payment information are provided for global clients.

Do you provide domain and hosting?

Yes. Domain registration/setup and hosting support can be included with a website project.

What payment methods are available?

India clients can use supported Cashfree payment methods. International clients can be provided verified ACH/bank-transfer instructions through an official invoice.

Is GST charged?

For India, applicable taxes/GST are charged according to the applicable tax status and invoice. If the business is registered and the service is taxable, the applicable GST is shown on the invoice. International tax treatment depends on applicable law.

Can I request revisions?

Yes. Revisions are handled according to the package and written project scope.
