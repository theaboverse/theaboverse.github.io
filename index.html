FAQ

Questions Global Clients Usually Ask

Is the 50% advance refundable?

The standard project policy is that the 50% advance is non-refundable once the project has been accepted and work has started, subject to the applicable written agreement and consumer laws.

When is the remaining 50% payable?

The remaining 50% is payable after the agreed project milestone, review or work approval, as specified in the project agreement.

How long does delivery take?

Delivery time depends on project scope, number of pages, features, content and client feedback. A delivery timeline should be confirmed before the project begins.

Do you work with international clients?

Yes. ARB Globle IT & Service is designed to serve clients in India and international markets.

Which payment methods are available?

India clients may use supported Cashfree payment methods such as UPI/cards where enabled. International clients may use approved global payment or bank-transfer methods subject to availability.

Is GST applicable?

Applicable Indian taxes should be calculated according to the customer's location, transaction structure and applicable law. Do not assume a fixed tax treatment without the required business and tax details.

Can I request revisions?

Yes. Revisions can be requested according to the number of revisions and scope agreed in the project package or written project agreement.

Can ARB connect my existing domain?

Yes. Existing domains can generally be connected to the new website subject to registrar and DNS access.
